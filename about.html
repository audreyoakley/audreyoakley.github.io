Meet Audrey's Bakery

What began as a weekend baking hobby has grown into a warm neighborhood bakery dedicated to handcrafted pastries, caring service, and joyful celebrations.

Our Story

From a home kitchen to your favorite neighborhood bakery

A while ago

The First Oven

Audrey opened the bakery with a simple goal: make every recipe better than the last. Starting with just a few signature pastries, word of mouth spread quickly through the neighborhood.

Not too long ago

Growing the Recipe Book

With loyal customers and endless creativity, the menu expanded to include cakes, pies, seasonal specialties, and custom celebration orders for every occasion.

Today!

Baking with the Same Care

We still bake with the same attention and trusted ingredients — only now, we get to share it with even more families, celebrations, and sweet moments.

Our Values

What guides every batch we bake

Community

We believe a bakery is the heart of a neighborhood. Every treat is made to bring people together — for celebrations, quiet mornings, and everything in between.

Quality

Trusted ingredients, time-honored techniques, and a keen eye for detail. We never cut corners because your taste deserves the very best.

Kindness

From the way we greet you at the door to the care in every box we wrap, kindness is the secret ingredient in everything we do.

Why Audrey's?

We blend classic techniques with modern favorites, and we love helping customers celebrate milestones with thoughtful, beautiful baked goods.
